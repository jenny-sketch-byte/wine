# Sentence Templates — Fill-in-the-Blank Answer Writing

Purpose: stop composing sentences from scratch under time pressure. Fill the blanks instead.
These templates match how WSET markers expect written answers to be shaped (factor → effect → outcome).

## The core template (use for almost any "explain" question)

> "[Region/practice] has/experiences **[factor]**, which causes/leads to **[mechanism]**, resulting in **[effect on grape/wine]**. This means the wine shows **[style/quality outcome]**."

Example (Alsace):
> "Alsace is protected by the Vosges Mountains, which causes a rain shadow effect, resulting in low rainfall and reduced disease pressure. This means grapes can hang longer on the vine, allowing a range of styles from dry to late harvest."

## Climate → wine style

> "The [cool/warm/hot] climate in [region] means grapes ripen [slowly/quickly], resulting in [high/low] natural acidity and [high/low] sugar levels at harvest."

> "Because [region] has a [maritime/continental/Mediterranean] climate, vintage variation is [high/low], which affects [consistency/typicity] from year to year."

## Viticulture practice → quality

> "[Practice, e.g. canopy management/low yields/hand harvesting] is used in [region] to [effect, e.g. increase sun exposure/concentrate flavour], which improves [ripeness/quality/consistency]."

## Winemaking choice → style

> "[Technique, e.g. extended maceration/lees ageing/malolactic conversion] is used to [effect], giving the wine [characteristic, e.g. more tannin/richer texture/softer acidity]."

## Ageing / oak → style

> "Ageing in [new/old] oak of [size] for [duration] introduces [tannin/toast/vanilla/oxidative notes], which [adds structure/softens the wine/preserves freshness]."

## Comparing two regions/wines

> "Both [A] and [B] share [similarity], however [A] differs because [factor], resulting in [difference in style]. This is due to [underlying cause, e.g. climate/soil/regulation]."

## Business of wine / pricing

> "[Factor, e.g. hand-harvesting/low yields/oak ageing/transport] increases the cost of production, which is reflected in the final price because [reason]."

## Closing a "so what" — attach this to almost any factual statement
> "...which matters for quality/style because ___."
> "...meaning the winemaker can/must ___."
> "...which explains why this region is suited to ___."

## Vocabulary to reach for instead of vague words

| Avoid (vague) | Use instead |
|---|---|
| "good" | balanced, well-structured, concentrated, complex, age-worthy |
| "a lot of" | pronounced, high, significant |
| "not much" | subtle, low, restrained |
| "makes it better" | improves ripeness / concentrates flavour / adds structure / preserves acidity |
| "because of weather" | due to [specific factor: rainfall / diurnal range / sunshine hours / frost risk] |

## Drill routine
For each new topic: write ONE answer using the core template within 5 minutes of finishing the content review. Speed over polish — this is muscle memory training, not final-draft writing.
