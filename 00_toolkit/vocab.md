# Exam & Wine Vocabulary Glossary

English terms that matter for WSET writing — not synonyms, the exact words examiners expect.
Add to this every session.

## General wine terms

| Term | Meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sediment / deposit | Solid particles that settle out of wine over time (tartrate crystals, dead yeast, pigments, tannins) | Different from "lees" — lees is specifically yeast residue from fermentation, often deliberate (sur lie) |
| Lees | Dead yeast cells remaining after fermentation | Can be aged on deliberately for texture/flavour (sur lie ageing) |
| Diurnal range/variation | The difference between daytime high and nighttime low temperature | Large diurnal range = warm days build sugar, cool nights preserve acidity |
| Rain shadow | Area with reduced rainfall due to a mountain range blocking moisture-bearing winds | Key mechanism for Alsace, Mendoza |
| Botrytis (noble rot) | A fungus that, under the right conditions (morning humidity + afternoon sun), shrivels grapes and concentrates sugar/flavour without spoiling the wine | Different from grey rot (same fungus, but harmful — occurs in wet/humid conditions without drying) |
| Deficit irrigation | Deliberately withholding water at controlled points in the growing season | Used to concentrate flavour and control yield/berry size |

## SAT (Systematic Approach to Tasting) descriptor words — precision matters

| Vague | Use instead |
|---|---|
| strong smell | pronounced intensity |
| weak smell | light/medium(-) intensity |
| a bit sour | high acidity |
| smooth tannins | fine-grained / soft tannins |
| rough tannins | coarse / grippy / high tannins |
| long taste | long finish |
| short taste | short finish |
