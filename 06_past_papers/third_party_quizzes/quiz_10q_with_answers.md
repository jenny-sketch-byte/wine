# "Choose One Correct Answer" — 10 MCQs + Answer Key

Source: unattributed quiz page, "Choose one correct answer" header, 10 questions with a scored
answer key ("Evaluate your results!") and pass-grade thresholds. Transcribed verbatim; correct
option **bolded**.

---

**1. Which one of the following statements is correct about grape harvesting?**
a. The timing of the harvest is primarily influenced by the vineyard latitude.
b. Manual harvesting is essential for sparkling wine production.
c. Grapes harvested by hand do not require destemming.
**d. Machine harvesters are best suited for flat terrain.**

**2. Casablanca Valley is a sub-region that:**
a. experiences heavy rainfall. · b. is cooled by oceanic breezes. · **c. is located in Chile.** · d. is renowned for Merlot.

> ⚠️ Per the source's own answer key the correct option is (c) "is located in Chile" — note
> that (b) "is cooled by oceanic breezes" is also a true statement about Casablanca Valley; the
> source evidently intends (c) as the single best answer. Not reconciled further here.

**3. Which of the following regions are known for producing high-quality wines from Shiraz?**
a. Napa and Sonoma · **b. Barossa and Eden Valley** · c. Rioja and Ribera del Duero · d. Tuscany and Lombardy

**4. Which of the following statements about Burgundy wine is accurate?**
a. Wines from Chablis can be red or white.
**b. The white wines of Puligny-Montrachet are often oak-aged.**
c. Premier Cru vineyards in Burgundy are typically on hilltops.
d. Whole clusters are always used in Beaujolais winemaking.

**5. Which winemaking technique is used to enhance the extraction of color and tannin in red wine?**
**a. Pumping over** · b. Whole bunch fermentation · c. Open-top fermentation · d. Semi-carbonic maceration

**6. Which of the following statements about Valpolicella is accurate?**
a. Recioto della Valpolicella is a dry wine made from fresh grapes.
**b. Corvina is the dominant grape variety in Valpolicella.**
c. Amarone della Valpolicella is a sweet wine.
d. Valpolicella Ripasso is made by adding unfermented grapes to a finished wine.

**7. Which one of the following is closest in style to Pouilly-Fumé?**
**a. Sancerre** · b. Saumur · c. Chinon · d. Quincy

**8. Wines made from Nebbiolo typically have the following characteristics: COLOUR INTENSITY, TANNIN**
a. deep, low · b. deep, high · **c. pale, high** · d. medium, low

**9. Which of the following is a wine from Beaujolais?**
**a. Brouilly** · b. Mercurey · c. Chablis · d. Cahors

**10. Grapes used in the production of late harvest wines typically have the following characteristics:**
**a. high sugar, high acid** · b. low sugar, high acid · c. high sugar, low acid · d. low sugar, low acid
