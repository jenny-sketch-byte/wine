How the exam is marked

Quick glance: what's actually being tested, and how big a slice each part is.

This app covers this

Unit 1 · Theory

Closed-book, 2 hours. 50 multiple-choice questions plus 4 written questions worth 25 marks each.

Not covered by this app

Unit 2 · Tasting

Two wines, blind, 30 minutes. Assessed on the WSET Systematic Approach to Tasting Wine.

Both units must be passed independently at 55% or above to earn the qualification. A candidate who passes one and fails the other must resit only the failed unit.

By raw marks across every paper (191 total), theory is roughly four-fifths of the exam and tasting roughly a fifth. This is a rough guide to scale, not an official blended weighting: each unit and part still needs its own 55% to pass.
